During the **AWS First Cloud Journey** program, fundamental concepts of cloud computing were studied, along with hands-on experience deploying services on **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**.

The internship activities were organized into five weeks, covering account management, security, networking, computing, storage, serverless architecture, containers, and automatic scaling.

In addition to completing the practical labs, **CloudManga**, a cloud-based manga reading web application, was selected as the Capstone Project to apply the knowledge gained throughout the program.

## Weekly Worklog

**Week 1 (01/08 - 14/08):** [AWS Overview, AWS Console, Credits, and Budgets](1.1-Week1/)

**Week 2 (15/08 - 28/08):** [IAM, VPC, Amazon EC2, and Amazon S3](1.2-Week2/)

**Week 3 (29/08 - 11/09):** [AWS Lambda, CloudWatch, CloudTrail, and API Gateway](1.3-Week3/)

**Week 4 (12/09 - 19/09):** [Docker, Amazon ECS, Application Load Balancer, and Auto Scaling](1.4-Week4/)

**Week 5 (20/09 - 27/09):** [CloudManga Capstone Project and Final Review](1.5-Week5/)
