## Comparison of Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) and Results

During my participation in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I gained exposure to cloud computing through **AWS** practical exercises and the **CloudManga** Capstone project.

My learning outcomes are self-assessed according to the following standards:

| CLO | Objective | Achieved Results |
|---|---|---|
| **CLO1 – Apply Professional Knowledge** | Apply acquired knowledge to build practical applications | Gained initial experience using **Amazon S3**, **AWS Lambda**, **API Gateway**, **Cognito**, and **DynamoDB** to deploy CloudManga. |
| **CLO2 – Analyze and Solve Problems** | Identify and resolve problems that arise | Learned basic approaches to troubleshooting **CORS** errors, **IAM** permissions, and API connections. However, my ability to independently identify causes and resolve complex errors remains limited. |
| **CLO3 – Communication, Reporting, and Presentation** | Document and present the internship process | Created worklogs, architecture diagrams, and Workshop documentation. |
| **CLO4 – Professional Ethics and Safety** | Follow security principles and manage resources | Initially applied **IAM** permissions, used **Cognito** for authentication, and kept the **S3 Bucket** private. Further study of AWS security is needed. |
| **CLO5 – Teamwork and Collaboration** | Collaborate and receive feedback at work | Had opportunities to experience an AWS learning and practical environment. Since the project was mainly completed individually, my teamwork experience remains limited. |
| **CLO6 – Use Technical Tools** | Use tools that support system deployment | Gained experience using **AWS Management Console**, **Visual Studio Code**, and **GitHub**. I am not yet proficient in **AWS CLI** and automation tools. |
| **CLO7 – Self-Assessment and Career Development** | Identify limitations and develop a learning direction | Identified knowledge gaps in **Cloud Computing**, security, and application deployment, and established a direction for continued learning and practice. |

## Strengths

- Willing to learn and explore new technologies.
- Completed basic practical exercises and built the **CloudManga** application.
- Gained an initial understanding of how to connect a frontend with **AWS Serverless** services.
- Have experience using **Visual Studio Code**, **GitHub**, and creating technical documentation.

## Areas for Improvement

- My **AWS** knowledge remains at a basic level, and I do not yet have a deep understanding of how many services operate.
- My ability to troubleshoot complex errors is still limited, and I need to refer to documentation and tutorials.
- I do not yet have experience with **CI/CD**, **Infrastructure as Code**, and automated testing.
- My teamwork experience in real-world projects is limited.
- My time management skills need improvement, as some tasks were completed close to their deadlines.

## Next Steps (3–6 Months)

- Review fundamental **AWS** knowledge, especially **IAM**, **S3**, **EC2**, and **Lambda**.
- Continue practicing with small projects to improve independent problem-solving skills.
- Learn more about **Docker**, **GitHub Actions**, and automated deployment processes.
- Consider following the **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner** learning path after gaining a solid understanding of the fundamentals.

## Conclusion

The internship helped me develop foundational knowledge of **AWS** and begin applying it to the **CloudManga** project.

However, my current abilities are still mainly at an intermediate level. I need to continue practicing, strengthen my programming knowledge, and gain more deployment experience to work more independently in the future.
